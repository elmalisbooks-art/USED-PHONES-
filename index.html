🛡️ Safety tips
  • Meet in a busy public place during daylight.
  • Test calls, cameras, charging, speakers, Wi-Fi, and SIM.
  • Check the IMEI and make sure the phone isn't account-locked.
  • Never share passwords, PINs, or verification codes.
  • Avoid paying deposits before inspecting the phone.
